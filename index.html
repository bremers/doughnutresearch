Data obtained using
genuine humans.

Tired of low-quality data and increasing amounts of bots?
By controlling the conditions under which data is created, we collect data from real, attentive, verified people. Not unauthorized technologies.

We are developing two primary avenues of reaching participants:

On their own device

Verified once in person, then re-authenticated by biometrics with real-time attention tracking. Participants fill out surveys for pay in the privacy of their home or when they're out and about.

On hardware we control

Placed where people already gather, such as a campus, a café or a library, for controlled studies. Participants earn money while taking a break or waiting for their appointment.

We need partners!

  • Could your organization host us to collect data in your space, anywhere that people already gather?
  • Does your organization need verified human data now, from surveys to AI evaluation data?
